A small, dismissible pop-up that points visitors to the free ScamRead scam checker.

== Description ==

Shows a card in the bottom corner of scamread.com inviting visitors to try
https://app.scamread.com. It appears once per visit after a short delay. If a
visitor closes it, it stays hidden for them for 7 days (30 days after they
click through). The choice is kept in the visitor's own browser; nothing is
stored on the server and nothing is loaded from other sites.

Settings → ScamRead Checker: switch it on/off, change the delay, the
"hide for" days and the checker address. Purge the page cache after changes.
